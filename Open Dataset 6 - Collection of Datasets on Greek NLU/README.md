**OYXOY v1.0** is a **collection of datasets for Greek Natural Language Understanding (NLU)**.  
Its aim is to serve as a **community meeting point** for the **collective development, testing, and distribution** of resources for the evaluation of **Greek NLP systems**.

## Dataset Overview

- **Scope:** Greek Natural Language Understanding (NLU)  
- **Version:** 1.0  
- **Contributors:** Konstantinos Kogkalidis & Stergios Chatzikyriakidis  
- **Purpose:** Evaluation and benchmarking of Greek NLP systems  
- **Community-driven:** Designed to encourage collaboration and dataset sharing
