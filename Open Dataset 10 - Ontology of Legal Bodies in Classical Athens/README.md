**Ontology of Legal Bodies in Classical Athens v1.0** is an *ontoterminology* (a terminology whose conceptual system is a formal ontology, Roche 2007) defining the **primary legal bodies in Classical Athenian courts (419–323 BC)**.  

A *legal body* is defined as *“an individual or group who either presides over or judges a legal proceeding”*.  
The ontology draws upon the **corpus of Attic oratory**, the *Pseudo-Aristotelian Athenian Constitution*, and *The Law of Ancient Athens* by David D. Phillips.

## Downloads & Usage
- **Ontology file (RDF/OWL):** Import into [Protégé](https://protege.stanford.edu/) (open access ontology editor) to explore ontology metrics.  
- **Visualization:** Import into [VOWL](https://service.tib.eu/webvowl/) to generate graphical visualizations.  
- **SPARQL endpoint:** The `.rdf` file is queryable via a [SPARQL](https://sparql.org/sparql.html) endpoint.  
- **Ontodictionaries (HTML):**
  - **Terms (English)**  
  - **Terms (Ancient Greek)**
