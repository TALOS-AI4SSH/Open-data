**Version 1.0 of an ontoterminology** that models **events of the Hellenistic World (323–31 BC)**.  

This period spans from the **death of Alexander the Great** to the **Battle of Actium**, covering the **Wars of the Successors**, the establishment of the **Hellenistic Kingdoms and Dynasties**, and the growing **Roman influence in the Mediterranean**.  

The ontology includes the most important **agents (persons)** of the time, linked with the **events** that shaped history, and further connects them with their **primary sources** and the **authors** who chronicled them.

This dataset was built with **Tedi ontoTerminology Editor**, a software platform for the creation and management of ontoterminologies.
